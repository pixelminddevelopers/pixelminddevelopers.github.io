01

Overview

PixelMind Developers is committed to building long-term relationships with our clients through transparency and fairness. This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are evaluated for our custom software, automation, and AI development services.

Because every engagement is different, refund requests are assessed individually rather than through a fixed formula.

02

Project-Based Services

PixelMind Developers primarily provides custom digital services, including:

Website Development Mobile App Development AI Automation AI Chatbots AI Agents Software Engineering UI/UX Design

Because these services involve dedicated time, planning, and custom work tailored to your project, refund requests are reviewed individually rather than governed by a one-size-fits-all rule.

03

Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds may be considered when:

  • No substantial work has begun on your project.
  • A duplicate payment was made in error.
  • A billing error occurred.
  • PixelMind Developers is unable to begin the agreed project for reasons within its control.

Each request is reviewed individually against the circumstances of the engagement.

04

Non-Refundable Situations

To keep our process fair for both clients and our team, refunds are generally not available once:

  • Development work has started.
  • Project milestones have been completed.
  • Digital deliverables have been provided.
  • Delays are caused by missing client feedback, content, or approvals.
  • The project scope changes after work has commenced.

We understand that plans change, and we're always happy to discuss options for adjusting scope or timeline going forward.

05

Project Cancellation

Either party may choose to cancel a project. Where cancellation occurs after work has begun, any completed work remains billable according to the agreed proposal. We'll work with you to determine what has been delivered and what, if anything, remains outstanding.

06

Delayed Projects

Project timelines can be affected by factors such as:

  • Client response times
  • Third-party dependencies
  • Technical constraints
  • Force majeure events, or circumstances beyond reasonable control

These situations do not automatically qualify for a refund, though we remain committed to communicating clearly and working toward a fair resolution together.

07

Digital Deliverables

Once digital deliverables — such as source code, designs, deployed features, or completed milestones — have been provided or made accessible to you, they are generally considered fulfilled and are not eligible for a refund, in the same way physical or downloaded digital products typically cannot be returned once delivered.

08

Exceptional Circumstances

We recognize that every project is unique. PixelMind Developers may review refund requests outside the situations described above on a case-by-case basis, with the goal of reaching a fair and reasonable outcome for everyone involved.

09

Policy Updates

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or business practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the most recent revision was made. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
